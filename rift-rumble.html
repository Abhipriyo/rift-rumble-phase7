WIPED OUT!

AI LAB

Every number here is calculated live in this browser from your own play. If something cannot be measured yet, it says so. Your moves never leave the page; only announcer recaps go to a language model, and only when you switch it on.

Move predictor

A sequence model (n-gram with backoff) predicts your next action. Hunter enemies position themselves against its guess. Evaluation set: every action you take this run, each predicted before it happens.

Next move guess: unavailable

Shadow Clone network

A neural network (13 inputs, 24 hidden units, 10 actions) learns to copy your decisions. Every 5th example is held out and never trained on, so the agreement below is measured on decisions it has not seen.

Limits: it copies 10 coarse actions from 13 numbers describing the situation, sampled 10 times a second. Agreement measures imitation, not fighting skill. Held-out examples come from your own sessions, so this is not a test on new players.
